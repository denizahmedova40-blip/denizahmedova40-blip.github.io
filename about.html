At ADA University, I am continuously growing both technically and personally, taking on challenges that push me beyond the classroom. Outside of academics, I stay actively involved in the tech community through hackathons and student organizations, and I'm committed to contributing to my community through volunteering. I am fluent in Azerbaijani, English, Turkish, and Russian, which allows me to collaborate and connect with people from diverse backgrounds.
